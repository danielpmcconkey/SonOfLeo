# Stay Within the Statement of Position

**Source:** Audit 2026-07-06a — CUST-6/8/9 overrules, skill improvement item #105a

Dan provides a statement of position at the start of each audit. It defines what's built and what isn't. Judge against that statement and the current CLI surface. Do not flag capability gaps in domains that don't exist yet. Do not audit future plans against the vision statement.

## What went wrong

- CUST-6 flagged that obligations and portfolio weren't on any roadmap list. Dan: "I'm not gonna pay for you to pretend to be a project manager."
- CUST-8 flagged no batch posting. The staging domain (not yet built) will address this. Dan: "Why are we poking holes in future plans that don't exist?"
- CUST-9 speculated about ML/Monte Carlo needs without knowledge of the existing PersonalFinance project.

## The rule

Mid/far horizon observations are advisory context, not findings. A finding must be grounded in something that exists today or something Dan said he's building next. "When you build X, you'll need Y" is advisory. "X doesn't exist" when Dan never said it should is not a finding.
