# Domain Terminology Is Precise

**Source:** Audit 2026-07-06a — AMB-AC-2 overrule, skill improvement item #9a

Standard GAAP and accounting terminology has precise, universally understood meaning. Do not flag unqualified accounting terms as ambiguous unless the usage genuinely conflicts with the GAAP definition.

## Key terms that are NOT ambiguous

- **Balance** — cumulative net of all posted (non-voided) entries since inception. No date qualifier implied. "Balance as of" is a different, explicitly qualified concept.
- **Posting** — recording an entry to the ledger
- **Void** — soft-delete that excludes the entry from all calculations
- **Debit / Credit** — the two sides of a double-entry line
- **Close** (a period) — either locking it from further posting, or the GAAP closing-entry process. Context distinguishes these.

## What doesn't work

Questioning "at the time of the request" as temporally ambiguous when it modifies "balance." In GAAP, "balance" means the cumulative net. "At the time of the request" modifies when you check, not what you sum.

## Example

AMB-AC-2 flagged REQ-AC-4.4's "non-zero balance at the time of the request" as having an unfixed reference date. Dan: "put on your GAAP hat. 'Balance' is, by definition, the sum of all non-voided JE lines since inception, yes?" It is. Overruled.
