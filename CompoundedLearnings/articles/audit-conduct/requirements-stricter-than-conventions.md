# Requirements May Be Stricter Than Learnings

**Source:** Audit 2026-07-06a — CQ-8 overrule, skill improvement item #48a

A behavioral requirement that is narrower than a general principle stated in a learning is not a contradiction. Requirements elaborate general guidance for specific domains.

## Example

A general principle says the split residual goes to "one of the resultant parts." REQ-MON-2.4.5 pins it to "the first share only." The requirement is stricter — and that's correct. The learning states the principle; the requirement pins it for the Money domain.

## The rule

Learnings are general. Requirements are specific. A requirement can narrow a learning without contradicting it. Only flag a contradiction when the requirement *violates* the learning (allows something the learning forbids), not when it *restricts* it.
