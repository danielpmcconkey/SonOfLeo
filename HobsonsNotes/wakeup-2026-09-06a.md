## What happened this session

### The Saturday pipeline conversation

Dan brought Simian's step-by-step breakdown of the post-projectionSweep
flow. Hobson and Dan hashed out business logic:

- **Classification is row-focused; PA resolution is rule-focused.** The
  dangerous failure is one rule claiming two entries (double attribution),
  not one entry matching two rules. The pivot flips the axis.
- **Operator review gate** between PA classification and payment matching.
  Hobson reviews, corrects, signs off. Point of no return.
- **Overpayment** — flagged, not automated. No `Overpaid` state. Under
  GAAP the excess is a credit we can't model yet. Park for operator.
- **No backstop creation** — hard stop with rollback if a linked entry
  has no instance or invoice. Confirmed as a requirement.

### The five-whys restructuring

Simian hit compile-order walls trying to build routes: types couldn't
move to CashFlowComponent because Classification sits downstream,
converters couldn't move to CashFlowFieldConverters for the same reason.
Dan and Simian traced it to the root: classification and cash flow were
offloading their bookkeeping onto `StageEntryLine` for convenience.

**The fix — three tables, three concerns:**

1. **Diagnostic table** (owned by StageDataClassification) — "rule X
   matched entry Z." Immutable. Classifier's audit trail.
2. **PA linkage table** (owned by CashFlow) — "PA Y claims entry Z."
   SOR for the match decision. Written by CashFlowOps for clean matches,
   corrected by operator, read by ProcessObligations.
3. **Payment records** (owned by CashFlow) — "Invoice I is satisfied by
   entry Z." Created by ProcessObligations.

`paymentAgreementId` on `StageEntryLine` is eliminated.
`accountClassificationRuleId` and `paymentClassificationRuleId` also
eliminated. `accountId` stays — it's the ingestion domain's own business.

### State machine revised

One classification step (2.2.5.0) runs both passes under one run ID.
Ingest (2.2.2.0) is pure ingestion, no classification. Account
resolution (2.2.6.0) and PA resolution (2.2.7.0) are separate consumers
of the same diagnostic run. PA resolution creates linkage records for
clean matches; operator reviews at 2.2.7.1; ProcessObligations at
2.2.8.0 reads the linkage table.

### Four determinism requirements added

1. Deterministic sort on every operator-facing return
2. Idempotency via unique constraints (linkage and diagnostic tables)
3. One clock per Saturday run (Context instant, not Calendar.today())
4. Re-run after partial success must be safe (load-bearing for F.4.0.0)

### PA linkage gets SonOfLeo's first hard delete

Confirmed by Dan and Hobson. The no-delete convention is GAAP/ledger,
not universal. An operational belief that's wrong gets removed, not voided.
The diagnostic table is the audit trail.
