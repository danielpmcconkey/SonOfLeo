## What's next

**Dan is implementing Option 4 for staged entry status.**

The decision: remove the `status` column from `ingestion.staged_entry`.
The audit trail (`ingestion.staged_entry_audit`) becomes the single
source of truth. Every read query derives current status from the latest
audit row via a CTE or join — the `all_statuses` pattern already used
in `fetchFiltered` becomes the standard.

Why Option 4 over the alternatives:
- Option 1 (code encapsulation): security theater — `internal` doesn't
  hold within the project
- Option 2 (DB trigger): real teeth, but puts business logic in the
  database; `change_mechanism` and `from_status` are awkward in a trigger
- Option 3 (remove from model type): punishing — derive on every read
  when you control the write path
- Option 4 (remove from table, derive on read): one source of truth in
  DB, one derivation point in read path, all business logic in F#

What Hobson needs to do after Dan's implementation:
- Update REQ-STG-2.7 (status cannot be null) — still true, but now
  derived not stored
- Update REQ-STG-2.24 — may need rewording or withdrawal since the
  denormalization it describes will no longer exist
- Review any other REQs that reference the status column directly
