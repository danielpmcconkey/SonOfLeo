**Thread 1 — Journal Entry spec, COMPLETE.** Reworked top to bottom from BD's
draft. The headline reversal: BD deferred references and voiding as "rarely used
/ never exercised" — **prod data (leobloom_prod) proved the opposite: references
on 97% of entries (1186/1221), voids on 10% (122).** So both came back. Final
model:
- **External references** (child table): `source_fi` + `reference`, audit-only.
  Dedup moved OFF the ledger to the importer/stage layer.
- **Comments** (child table): free text + nullable secondary-JE link = the
  correction/relationship mechanism. Reversal is NOT a separate thing — it's an
  offsetting entry + a comment.
- Voiding = soft-delete marker; reason lives in a comment; closed-period void
  rejected; re-void errors (REQ-SYS-6.1, the new no-op-rejection rule).
- Dan then made his own pass and is **satisfied with the requirements**: struck
  the `source_fi` controlled-vocab (1.43) and write-once (1.47), added 4.9
  (amend a ref's FI/value), 5.5 (comment on voided/closed JE), 1.29 (line→JE FK),
  bumped description/memo to 1000 chars.

**Thread 2 — Account active period Instant → LocalDate, DONE & GREEN.** Account
`active_begin`/`active_end` are now Calendar Dates, **inclusive both ends**
(`begin <= ref <= end`). Reasoning: they're business boundaries compared only
against day-granular entry dates; sub-day precision is unobservable (same logic
that killed leap seconds), and it unifies them with the fiscal-period and
entry-date types so posting validation is pure date comparison, zero conversion.
All comparison operators flipped correctly (`<=`→`<` on the reject side, `>`→`>=`
on the active side), tests updated and passing, including the subtle catch that
inclusive-end means "deactivate as of today" leaves the account active *through*
today. New `Utilities/Calendar.fs` is the one surviving Instant→Eastern-date
bridge (`dateFromInstant`, `today()`), zone `America/New_York`.
