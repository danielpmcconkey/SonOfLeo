## What happened this session

### CashFlow domain design (two-day conversation, 2026-08-26 through 08-28)

Major design arc, starting from scratch and arriving at the final entity
model through iterative refinement:

1. **Entity model evolution:** Started with Agreement + ExpectedTransaction +
   Instance + Transaction. Through several rounds of "sleep on it and
   redesign," arrived at the final five-entity model:
   - **Master Agreement** (Template) — contract, cadence, counterparty
   - **Payment Agreement** (Template) — one JE leg: debit/credit accounts
   - **Instance** (Event) — one period occurrence
   - **Invoice** (Event) — amount due per leg per period, owns lifecycle
   - **Payment** (Event) — receipt: cash moved, links to JE or staged entry

2. **Key design decisions:**
   - **Template vs Event** categorization — the organizing principle
   - **Invoice owns the lifecycle** (invoice state, payment state, posted
     state, blocker) — Payment is a receipt, not a lifecycle tracker
   - **Invoice state is direction-dependent** — Income: Generated/Sent;
     Outgo: Expected/Received. Validation in `InvoiceState.isValidFlowDirectionInvoiceStateCombination`
   - **TransactionPointer DU** (`Posted of JeHeaderId | Staged of SeHeaderId`)
     — required on Payment. DB has two nullable columns; model has one DU.
     Both populated is the normal end state.
   - **Payment amount is derived** from the JE or staged entry line, not
     stored. Flow direction determines which side to match: Income → credit
     account/Credit line; Outgo → debit account/Debit line.
   - **One line per leg per entry** — documented assumption. No aggregation
     in the amount derivation query. Fan-out from a defective JE is the
     correct failure mode.

3. **Classification authority hierarchy** (new, for SonOfLeo Saturday):
   0. Dan — override authority, rarely exercised
   1. Hobson — judgment authority when operating as comptroller
   2. Parser — hard-wired account assignments
   3. Classification rule — pattern matching

4. **Saturday routine redesign** — obligations weigh in BEFORE posting:
   ingest → dedup → classify → **projection sweep** → **obligation routine**
   → classify unknowns → shadow post → post → update Payments → reconcile
   → projection → reports → summary. Written up in
   `HobsonsNotes/saturday-routine-sonofleo-draft-2026-08-28.md`.

5. **Spec written:** `Specs/Behavioral/CashFlow.md` — sections 1–8.
   Data states for all five entities (§2–6), projection sweep (§7),
   cash-flow projection (§8). No CRUD behavior sections yet.

6. **Portfolio lot-level tracking** — future note recorded at
   `HobsonsNotes/portfolio-lot-level-tracking-2026-08-28.md`. Track at
   lot level for taxable accounts (Monte Carlo needs cost basis per lot
   for long-term/short-term capital gains). Retirement accounts (401k,
   IRAs) stay aggregate.
