- Most of STE doesn't transfer. The controlled 900-word dictionary and sentence-length caps

- The sharpened principle that came out of it: **prefer the executable form of a rule;
  where it can't be executable, write it once, as close as possible to the thing it
  governs.** Everything that rotted is precisely the material nothing executes. Nine
  `Checks/` scripts are green and none are stale, because a check that goes stale *fails*.
