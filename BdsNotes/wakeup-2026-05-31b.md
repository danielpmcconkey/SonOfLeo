## Open design questions (parked)

1. **AccountSubtype valid-for-type enforcement:** Where does the rule live
   that says Cash is only valid for Asset, OperatingExpense only for
   Expense, etc.? Two options discussed:
   - **Option A (Model layer):** `Account` gets a private constructor +
     `create` smart constructor that validates the subtype/type pairing.
     Purer — if you have an `Account`, it's guaranteed valid.
   - **Option B (Ledger layer):** The service validates before persisting.
     Simpler but relies on discipline.
   - Dan was leaning A but hadn't decided. BD leaned A too (learning
     project, lean into the paradigm).

2. **UUID scope:** Does UUID apply to all entity PKs or just `account`?
   Not decided yet.

3. **`parentId` type:** Currently `int option` — needs to become UUID
   option once the ID decision is implemented.
