  ("this site enforces REQ-X"), not the ability to name a rule.

  `CompoundedLearnings/articles/process/release-candidate-merge-flow.md`. BD verifies each
  branch and hands it over; Dan reviews; at session end BD assembles an RC and verifies
  that; Dan merges the RC. BD never touches `main`.

  archetypes get decided and presented *with* the stubs, not discovered mid-implementation.
