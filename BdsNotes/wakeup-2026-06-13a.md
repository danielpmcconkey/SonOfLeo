Two big tracks: NodaTime wiring completion and convention doc blitz.

**NodaTime / temporal:**
- Fixed DAL.fs: data source builder with `UseNodaTime()`, RowReader switched from
  `DateTimeOffset` to `Instant`, parameter mapping uses `NpgsqlDbType.TimestampTz`.
- Killed `IClock` injection pattern. Replaced with `AuditEnvelope` — a domain type in
  Model that captures the operation instant once at the front door and threads it through
  all write operations. Account.fs fully rewired to take `AuditEnvelope` instead of `IClock`.
- Clock.fs simplified to a convenience function (`Clock.now()`) that reads
  `DateTimeOffset.UtcNow`, converts to `Instant`, and truncates to microsecond precision
  for lossless Postgres round-trips. Fixed a value-vs-function binding bug (missing parens).
- Resolved the Npgsql.NodaTime namespace collision (it's a package name, not a namespace).
- Deleted the "UTC connection string" convention — unnecessary with `UseNodaTime()`.
- Deleted Temporal.md leftovers (old IClock bullet, truncation bullet, timestamptz-only
  bullet, broken tstzrange bullet). The tstzrange/Interval bullet was wrong — fiscal
  periods are date ranges, not instant ranges.

**Money type:**
- New `Money.fs` in Model: private decimal wrapper enforcing penny precision at
  construction. `fromDecimal` validates 2dp and min/max bounds matching `numeric(12,2)`.
  `add`, `subtract` route through `fromDecimal` for overflow protection. `splitByN` splits
  into N parts with residual forced into the first element. `fromDecimalList` uses
  `foldBack` to sequence `Result` values.
- Negative amounts are legal (double-entry).
- Money.md rewritten with arithmetic conventions: unpack to decimal for multiplication/
  division, repack through Money constructor, half-up rounding, exact allocation.
