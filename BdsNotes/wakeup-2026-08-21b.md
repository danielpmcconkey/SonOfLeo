### Mutation evidence

Every assertion in all 55 perturbed, run, failure read, reverted — **ten passes**, because
several assertions sit behind an earlier one and never get their turn in a single pass. Two
techniques worth reusing:

- For the five numeric operators, forcing the shared `amountMatches` helper to a constant
  `true` and then a constant `false` puts all 15 assertions red across two runs. Cleaner than

- A mutation run that goes **all** red tells you less than one that doesn't. In the first
  `FieldMatchEvaluation` pass one test came back green — two of my string replacements had both
  matched the same assertion and cancelled each other. The survivor is the signal.
