## SD-2 — statement-delta
- **Location:** Src/Business.CrossDomainOrchestration/StageEntryOrchestration.fs `post` (lines 803-835); Src/Business.CrossDomainOrchestration/CashFlowOps.fs `transitionPaymentsToPosted` (line 886); Src/Ui.InterfaceBridge/Routes/CashFlowRoutes.fs (lines 29-32, 162); Specs/Behavioral/CashFlow.md §10 (REQ-CF-10.1..10.7)
- **Summary:** Dan says a Payment gains its journal entry line linkage when its staged entry posts, but posting does not touch Payments. A separate operator command does it later.
- **Resolution:** dan-decides

Statement: 'A Payment is created pointing at its staged line, and gains its journal entry line linkage when the staged entry eventually posts.' Repo: StageEntryOrchestration.post fetches the postable entries, posts each one (postStageEntry) and sets the header status to Posted. It never reads or updates Payments. The Payment's journal_entry_line_id is set only by CashFlowOps.transitionPaymentsToPosted (journalEntryLineIdUpdate = SetTo (Some journalEntryLineId), line 857). That is a separate route in CashFlowRoutes.fs (handler at line 162). Spec §10 calls it 'a deterministic batch operation that runs after staged entries have been posted to the ledger (Phase 7 of the Saturday routine)'. If that command is never run, a Payment's pointer stays Staged even after its line has posted. The rest of the claim checks out: the Payment is created with a Staged pointer to its line (REQ-CF-13.4), and the staged line is kept as provenance (REQ-CF-10.3).

**Action:** Dan updates his mental model: Payment-to-posted is its own Saturday step (REQ-CF-10) and posting does not do it. If he wants posting to trigger it automatically, that is a spec change for Hobson.

**Why:** In a fully agentic Saturday state machine, believing that posting links Payments could lead someone to drop the transition step. Payments and Invoice posted states would then silently stay Staged.
